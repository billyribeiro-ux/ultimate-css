Module 9 · Motion

@layer animations: Scroll, Motion, and Transitions

Motion belongs in its own layer — the highest in the stack — because motion is the concern most likely to be overridden for accessibility, and the one that benefits most from being centralized. This module teaches the modern motion primitives: @starting-style, scroll-driven animations, scroll-state container queries, scroll triggers, sibling-index() stagger, and view transitions.

Module goal

Build a complete motion system that lives in one layer, responds to prefers-reduced-motion in one place, and handles nine out of ten common animation needs without a single imperative JavaScript call. Understand where GSAP still belongs — and where it does not.
