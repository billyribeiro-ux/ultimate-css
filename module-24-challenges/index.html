CSS Challenges — Test Your Mastery

Theory is nothing without practice. 12 challenges, increasing in difficulty. Each shows you a target, gives you constraints, and drops you into the editor. No looking at answers until you've tried.

How it works

Each challenge gives you three things: a target visual (what the result should look like), a set of constraints (rules you must follow), and a starter in the editor (HTML provided, CSS for you to write). The difficulty ramps from beginner through expert to a final "boss" challenge that tests everything.

Difficulty scale

  • Beginner — Tests one concept in isolation
  • Intermediate — Combines 2-3 techniques
  • Advanced — Requires deep understanding and creative problem-solving
  • Expert — Production-quality, multi-system challenges
  • Boss — Everything at once, under constraints
