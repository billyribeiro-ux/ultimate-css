The Complete Guide to CSS Print Stylesheets

When users print your page, they deserve a clean, readable document — not a mess of navigation bars and broken layouts. This guide covers everything you need to know about making your web content print-ready.

Setting Up break-after: avoid

The foundation of any print stylesheet is the @media print block. Inside it, you control what shows, what hides, and how content flows across pages. Read the full specification for details.

Page Control break-before: page

With @page rules you control margins, size, and orientation. Combined with break properties, you get precise control over pagination — headings never orphan, figures never split.

The orphans and widows properties ensure at least 3 lines appear on each side of a page break, preventing the "dangling single line" problem that makes printed documents look unprofessional.
