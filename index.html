Philosophy

Every concept is introduced through the problem it solves, not through its syntax. The student feels the pain of the old way before they ever see the new way. This is the Dave DiCenso root-level method applied to CSS — understand the system deeply before memorizing surface patterns.

Browser support is called out explicitly in every lesson covering modern features. Lessons distinguish between production-ready, progressive enhancement, and experimental.
