Lesson 17.9 · Micro-Interactions

Card Hover, Press & Drag Feedback

Cards that lift on hover with translateY(-2px) and a growing shadow, press down on :active with scale(0.98), zoom their image inside overflow: hidden, reveal content with a slide-up, and use the card-link pattern with a stretched ::after.

The lift effect

On hover, the card lifts 2px (using translateY(-2px)) and its box-shadow grows. The shadow transitions from a tight, subtle shadow to a larger, softer one. This creates the illusion of the card "floating" off the page. The transition uses 200ms ease for both transform and shadow.

Press feedback

On :active, the card scales to 0.98 and the shadow shrinks back. This gives tactile feedback that the card has been "pushed down." The transition for active is shorter (100ms) to feel snappy and responsive.

Image zoom

The card image wrapper has overflow: hidden. On card hover, the image inside scales to 1.05 with a 400ms ease transition. This slow zoom creates a cinematic parallax feel. The overflow clipping prevents the image from breaking out of its container.

Content reveal

A description or action row below the card content slides up from translateY(0.5rem) and fades in on hover. This progressive disclosure shows more detail only when the user signals interest. It uses a 200ms ease with a 50ms delay to sequence after the lift.

The card-link pattern

The card's primary link gets a ::after pseudo-element with position: absolute; inset: 0, making the entire card surface clickable. This avoids wrapping the whole card in an anchor (which creates accessibility issues with nested interactive elements). Secondary actions get position: relative; z-index: 1 to sit above the stretched link.

Mini-exercise

Add a "bookmark" icon button in the top-right corner of the card that sits above the card-link (using position: relative; z-index: 1). Give it a scale bounce on click. Then create a "horizontal card" variant where the image is on the left (using grid-template-columns: 8rem 1fr) and the hover effects still work identically.
